Week 10

Self Psychology

Kohut’s self psychology recenters the therapeutic relationship around empathy, attunement, and the selfobject experience. For social workers, this is often the most intuitive theoretical home — it takes seriously the idea that people need to feel understood before they can change. Geist’s paper on the forward edge is a beautiful articulation of self psychology’s clinical optimism.

Reflection Prompts

  1. Think about a client who seems to need you to be a very specific kind of presence — endlessly patient, admiring, or calm. How might you understand that need through the lens of selfobject functions rather than as “neediness” or “dependence”?
  2. Geist writes about the “forward edge” — the part of the client that is reaching toward growth even when the presentation looks like regression. Can you identify a client on your caseload whose forward edge you might be missing?
  3. How does self psychology’s emphasis on empathic attunement sit alongside the demands of your agency or setting? Where do you feel the tension between being empathically present and being “productive” in the way your system requires?

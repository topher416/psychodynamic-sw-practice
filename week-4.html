Week 04

Ego Psychology

Ego psychology bridges Freud’s structural model with the question social workers actually care about: how do people adapt? Anna Freud and the ego psychologists shifted attention from the id to the ego’s capacity for defense, adaptation, and resilience — concepts that map directly onto strengths-based practice.

Reflection Prompts

  1. Think about a client whose coping strategies frustrate you — maybe they intellectualize, avoid, or deny. From an ego psychology perspective, what is that defense protecting them from? How might naming it as adaptive rather than pathological change the way you intervene?
  2. Danzer argues that ego psychology and strengths-based social work share common ground. Where do you see these two frameworks converging in your own practice? Where do they diverge?
  3. Anna Freud shifted psychoanalytic attention from the id to the ego — from what drives us to how we manage what drives us. Think about a moment in session when a client’s ego functioning shifted in real time — when a defense went up or came down. What did you notice, and what did you do with it?
